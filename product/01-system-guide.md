## Part 1 — The mechanism, in plain English

**PDRN** stands for *polydeoxyribonucleotide*. It is a fragment of DNA — most commonly derived from salmon, sometimes from plants like ginseng. In cosmetics it usually appears on the ingredient list as **sodium DNA** or **PDRN**.

What it is believed to do, based on the research cited by dermatologists:

- **Activate adenosine A2A receptors** — calming inflammation and supporting the skin's own repair signaling.
- **Support new blood-vessel formation** (angiogenesis) — better delivery of oxygen and nutrients to the skin.
- **Encourage collagen renewal and tissue repair** — the same pathway the skin uses after wounding.

That is why PDRN first became famous in *clinic*: Rejuran, the Korean "healing essence," is injected or applied after microneedling or laser — because those procedures create **delivery channels** into the deeper skin where PDRN can signal.

**The blunt truth:** topical PDRN without any delivery help largely sits on the surface. Topical PDRN *with* a healthy barrier, a chemical or physical delivery method, and a calm baseline is where people report the "glass skin" glow.

So the protocol has one job: **create the conditions where PDRN is not wasted.**

### What PDRN is NOT

- It is **not** a plumping hydrator. If your skin feels tight and dry, hydration is a different job.
- It is **not** an exfoliant. It does not resurface. Acids do that.
- It is **not** a replacement for sunscreen. Nothing is.
- It is **not** instant. Reports of visible change cluster around **weeks 3–8**, not days 1–3.

If a product promises otherwise, that product is selling hope.

## Part 2 — The Barrier Audit (do this before you buy a single active)

You grade five things. Total possible: 10 points.

| # | Check | Score 0 | Score 1 | Score 2 |
|---|-------|---------|---------|---------|
| 1 | **Tightness after cleansing** | Tight for hours | Tight 10 min | Comfortable |
| 2 | **Redness / flushing** | Visible most days | Occasional | Rare |
| 3 | **Reaction to new actives** | Burns/stings | Mild tingle | No reaction |
| 4 | **Texture (roughness, flaking)** | Flaky patches | Slightly rough | Smooth |
| 5 | **Stinging with water alone** | Yes | Sometimes | No |

**Your score:**

- **8–10 → Barrier is READY.** Start Phase 3 Activate this week.
- **5–7 → Barrier is REACTIVE.** Give Phase 1 Reset 7 days and Phase 2 Rebuild 14 days.
- **0–4 → Barrier is COMPROMISED.** You are in Reset for at least 21 days. No actives. No PDRN yet.

*Be honest. A flattering score costs you six weeks and the price of the serum you wasted.*

## Part 3 — The Four-Phase Protocol

### PHASE 1 — RESET (7–21 days)
**Goal: remove the insults. Stop the bleeding.**

Morning:
1. Rinse with lukewarm water only (no cleanser if you can tolerate it)
2. Simple moisturizer (ceramides, no fragrance, no essential oils)
3. Mineral SPF 30+ — non-negotiable

Evening:
1. Gentle non-foaming cleanser, 20 seconds, lukewarm
2. Simple moisturizer — apply to slightly damp skin
3. Nothing else. No acids, no retinol, no vitamin C, no PDRN, no clay, no scrubs, no tools.

Rules: no hot showers on the face, no washing more than twice a day, no new products, no "just one" exfoliation.

### PHASE 2 — REBUILD (14 days)
**Goal: rebuild the barrier's raw material while keeping irritation at zero.**

Add, one at a time, 5 days apart:
- **Ceramide + cholesterol + fatty-acid moisturizer** (the three barrier lipids)
- **Panthenol (B5) or madecassoside** — soothing support
- **Squalane or a light occlusive** at night

Still no actives. You are laying the mortar before you build the wall.

*Log every product in the Product Tracker (deliverable 5). One new product at a time, 5-day gap. This is how you find your triggers instead of guessing.*

### PHASE 3 — ACTIVATE (weeks 4–12)
**Goal: introduce PDRN and its delivery window.**

Now — and only now — you introduce PDRN.

**The PDRN Sandwich (the core move of this system):**

1. **Cleanse** gently, pat almost dry
2. **Hydrating toner / essence** — a wet base layer
3. **PDRN serum** — 2–3 drops, press in, do not rub aggressively
4. **Barrier moisturizer** — seal it

**Frequency ramp (do not skip this):**

- Week 1 of Phase 3: **2 nights only** (e.g. Mon + Thu)
- Week 2: **3 nights**
- Week 3: **4 nights**
- Week 4+: **5–6 nights**, or nightly if fully comfortable

**Optional delivery boosters (choose ONE, and only after two clean weeks):**
- A **PDRN mask** 1–2× week (overnight masks are the most accessible entry)
- **Microneedling at home**: extreme caution — see deliverable 7. Dermarolling yourself is the fastest way to undo everything. Prefer a professional, or skip it.
- **Laser/microneedling in clinic**: this is where PDRN shines — but recovery downtime is part of the deal.

**Keep it simple:** the sandwich, the ramp, and consistency beat any stack of 12 products.

### PHASE 4 — MAINTAIN (week 12+)
**Goal: hold your result without re-injuring the barrier.**

- PDRN 3–5 nights a week (your skin will tell you; 3 is plenty for most)
- One gentle acid, **1× week maximum**, only if your barrier score stays 8+
- Retinol: if you use it, put it on **separate nights** from PDRN. Never stack them.
- Re-run the Barrier Audit every 30 days.

## Part 5 — The STOP Rules

Pause everything (back to Phase 1) the moment any of these appear:

1. **Stinging with water alone.** Your barrier is open. Stop.
2. **New redness that lasts more than 24 hours.** That is inflammation, not "purging."
3. **Flaking that itches.** Not dryness — damage.
4. **A breakout of tiny uniform bumps.** Often a reaction, not acne.
5. **Any product that burns on application.** Burning is not "working."

When you pause: cleanse, moisturize, SPF, nothing else, for 7 days. Then re-run the Barrier Audit. Do not "push through." Barrier damage compounds.

## The honest disclaimer (read it)

This is **educational information**, not medical advice, and not a substitute for a dermatologist. PDRN research is still evolving — dermatologists themselves say "conclusions should be drawn carefully." Nothing here diagnoses or treats a condition. If you have rosacea, eczema, psoriasis, active acne, or an impaired barrier from a medical condition, talk to a board-certified dermatologist before starting any active. Patch test everything. Pause anything that hurts.

Skin repair is slow biology, not a fast hack. The protocol works because it respects that.
