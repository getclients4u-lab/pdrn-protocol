# The PDRN Product Cheat-Sheet

### How to tell a real PDRN product from a glow-washed bottle — in 30 seconds

---

## The 30-second rule

Flip the bottle. Find the ingredient list. Look for **one of these two words**:

> **SODIUM DNA** or **PDRN**

If it's there, the product actually contains PDRN. If it's not, "PDRN" on the front is decoration.

**That's the whole test.** Marketing text, "salmon DNA extract" on the front panel, and influencer claims do not count. Only the INCI list counts.

---

## Why this matters

PDRN is not a cheap ingredient, so many products include a token amount and lean on the label. Cosmetic chemists point out that ingredient lists don't even tell you **where the DNA fragments came from** or **how much is in there**. So:

1. **Check the INCI list** for *sodium DNA* / *PDRN*.
2. **Check the position** — ingredients are listed in descending order. If sodium DNA sits after the preservative, it's essentially a sprinkle.
3. **Check the delivery claim** — topical PDRN is most promising when paired with a delivery method (micronized delivery, a mask format, or use after professional microneedling/laser).

---

## The PDRN product map

| Format | Typical concentration story | Best for | Watch out for |
|--------|----------------------------|----------|---------------|
| **Ampoule / serum** | Highest, most concentrated | Daily Phase 3 use | Price inflation; verify INCI |
| **Overnight mask** | Medium, sits on skin for hours | Best accessible "delivery" | Fragrance on reactive skin |
| **Cream / moisturizer** | Lower, but barrier-friendly | Barrier-reactive skin, entry point | Weak PDRN dose |
| **Essence** | Low, watery layer | Layering in the Sandwich | Mostly water — don't overpay |
| **Clinic / ampoule after needling** | Highest efficacy window | Real visible change | Cost, downtime, pro required |

**Rule of thumb:** the *format's delivery* matters as much as the concentration. An overnight mask often outperforms a watery essence that just evaporates.

---

## Red flags (any one of these = walk away)

- 🚩 "PDRN" on the front, **no sodium DNA** in the list
- 🚩 Sodium DNA listed **dead last** (token amount)
- 🚩 Promises "salmon sperm facial results at home in 3 days"
- 🚩 **No ingredient list** published anywhere on the site
- 🚩 Heavy **fragrance / essential oils** marketed to "sensitive skin"
- 🚩 Bundled with a **microneedling roller** and told to "roll daily"
- 🚩 Claims it "replaces Botox / filler / laser"
- 🚩 No batch/lot info, no manufacturer address, no expiry

## Green flags

- ✅ **Sodium DNA** or **PDRN** in the INCI, listed before the preservative
- ✅ States the **source** (salmon-derived vs. plant/ginseng-derived)
- ✅ Honest about the **delivery caveat** ("best used after needling / in a mask")
- ✅ Fragrance-free or low-fragrance formula
- ✅ Full INCI published, batch code, expiry
- ✅ Refund policy that doesn't require an essay to claim

---

## Vegan vs. salmon-derived

**Salmon-derived PDRN** is the original and the most studied. **Botanical PDRN** (e.g. ginseng-derived) has early evidence for supporting regeneration and barrier function, and is the option if you avoid animal-derived ingredients. Neither is "wrong." Know which one you're buying — the label often won't say.

**You do not need the premium tier to start.** You need a barrier that's ready, a product that actually lists sodium DNA, and consistency for 8 weeks. The $26 option used correctly beats the $70 option used randomly.

## The 5-question buying checklist

Before you buy any PDRN product, answer:

1. Does the INCI list contain **sodium DNA / PDRN**? (yes → continue)
2. Is it listed **before the preservative** (phenoxyethanol / parabens)? (yes → continue)
3. Is it **fragrance-free** or low-fragrance? (yes → continue)
4. Does it fit a **format you'll actually use** (serum you'll layer / mask you'll do weekly)? (yes → continue)
5. Is your **barrier score 8+**? (yes → buy. No → go fix the barrier first.)

If you fail #5, buying more PDRN is throwing money at a wall with holes in it.

---

## What to do with your current bottle

**If you already own a PDRN product:**

- Check the INCI. Real PDRN? Good — it goes in the Sandwich, Phase 3, 2 nights to start.
- No sodium DNA? It's a hydrating essence. Use it as the wet layer in the Sandwich. No harm, no glow.
- Fragranced and your skin is reactive? Park it until your audit hits 8, then patch test.

**Never throw away a working barrier routine for a new active.** The bottle will wait. Your barrier won't.
