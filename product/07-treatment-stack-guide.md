# The Treatment Stack Guide

### Microneedling, laser, and in-clinic PDRN — what works, what's risky, and how to not wreck your face

---

## Why this guide matters

PDRN became famous *in a treatment room*. The Korean "healing essence" is applied or injected **after** microneedling or laser, because those procedures create the **delivery channels** that let PDRN reach the layer where it can signal.

Which means: if you want the *real* PDRN result, at some point you'll consider a treatment. This guide tells you how to think about it — safely, and without being sold something you don't need.

---

## 1. The delivery principle (understand this and everything else makes sense)

> **Topical PDRN without delivery help mostly stays on the surface. Delivery is the multiplier.**

There are four delivery tiers, from weakest to strongest:

| Tier | Method | Delivery strength | Risk |
|------|--------|-------------------|------|
| 1 | **Topical serum alone** | Low | Very low |
| 2 | **Overnight mask format** | Low–medium (occlusion, time) | Low |
| 3 | **At-home microneedling + topical** | Medium–high | **High if done wrong** |
| 4 | **In-clinic needling / laser + PDRN** | Highest | Professional, controlled |

The higher the tier, the more the barrier must be **already healthy**. A treatment on a compromised barrier is a wound on a wound.

---

## 2. At-home microneedling: the honest section

**The internet will tell you to buy a derma-roller for $12 and go at your face 3× a week. Do not.**

Why it goes wrong:

- **Needle depth** for home rollers is usually 0.2–0.5mm — not enough to deliver actives deeply, but enough to create micro-wounds
- **Bacteria:** reusable rollers are nearly impossible to sterilise; you are rolling bacteria into open channels
- **Over-frequency:** weekly rolling on the same areas causes chronic inflammation, not collagen
- **Redness payoff:** any short-term "glow" from rolling is usually swelling, not repair
- **Actives in open skin:** applying strong actives to needled skin = irritation and sometimes pigment damage

**If you decide to try it anyway, the guardrails:**

- Only after your barrier audit is **8+**
- **Stamp** device (not roller) with **new, sterile** cartridges
- **0.25mm maximum** at home; never deeper
- **Once every 10–14 days**, not weekly
- **Only** hydrating/PDRN products after — **no acids, no retinol, no vitamin C**
- Never on active acne, eczema, moles, or broken skin
- Stop at the first sign of persistent redness

**But the better call:** skip home needling, use an **overnight PDRN mask** for the occlusion effect, and put your money toward one professional treatment instead of twelve rollers.

## 3. In-clinic treatments: what to ask

If you go professional, you're looking at one of:

| Treatment | What it does | PDRN pairing | Downtime |
|-----------|--------------|--------------|----------|
| **Microneedling (RF or standard)** | Creates channels, stimulates collagen | PDRN applied immediately after | 1–3 days redness |
| **Fractional laser** | Resurfaces, deep collagen | PDRN post-procedure | 3–7 days |
| **Skin boosters / injections** | Delivers directly into dermis | PDRN or HA-based | 1–2 days |
| **"Salmon sperm facial" (Rejuran-style)** | Multiple shallow injections of PDRN | Is the delivery | 2–3 days bumps |

**Questions to ask before booking:**

1. What exact PDRN product do you use, and is it the salmon-derived or plant-derived version?
2. How many sessions does realistic change require? (usually 2–4, spaced 3–4 weeks)
3. What is the aftercare protocol — what can I put on my skin, and when?
4. Am I a candidate given my skin history (rosacea, keloid, active acne, cold sores)?
5. What does "no result" look like, and what's the plan if I don't see change?

**Red flags:** "one session and you're done," no patch test, no aftercare instructions, no discussion of your barrier history, pressure to prepay a package.

## 4. Pre-treatment checklist (2 weeks before)

- [ ] Barrier Audit score **8+**
- [ ] No active breakouts, cold sores, or open skin
- [ ] No acid or retinol for **5 days** before
- [ ] No sun exposure / no tanning **1 week** before
- [ ] No blood thinners or NSAIDs **24–48h** before (ask your provider)
- [ ] Hydrated, well-slept, no alcohol 48h before
- [ ] Patch test the post-care PDRN product **before** the treatment day

---

## 5. Post-treatment protocol (the part that decides your result)

**Days 0–3 — PROTECT**
- Gentle cleanser or water rinse only
- **PDRN + barrier moisturizer** — this is your entire routine
- Mineral SPF from day 1 (or as your provider directs)
- No actives, no acids, no retinol, no vitamin C, no exfoliation, no makeup if avoidable
- No gym, sauna, steam, swimming, or hot showers
- Sleep on a clean pillowcase

**Days 4–7 — CALM**
- Continue PDRN + moisturizer
- Add a soothing layer (panthenol/centella) if red
- Still no actives
- Keep out of direct sun

**Days 8–14 — REBUILD**
- Resume gentle products
- Barrier moisturizer twice daily
- Still no exfoliating acids or retinol for **at least 14 days** (ask your provider — some say longer)

**Week 3+ — MAINTAIN**
- Reintroduce actives one at a time, 5 days apart
- PDRN 3–5 nights/week
- SPF forever

## 6. The realistic expectation curve

| Timeline | What you'll actually see |
|----------|--------------------------|
| Days 1–3 | Redness, tightness, maybe swelling (that's the treatment, not the glow) |
| Week 1–2 | Skin looks "normal" again, maybe slightly dry |
| Week 3–4 | First hint of texture improvement — subtle |
| Week 4–8 | The change people post about: evenness, plumpness, "glass skin" |
| Week 8–12 | Collagen remodelling matures — this is the real result window |

**Anyone showing you a "before and after in 24 hours" is showing you swelling.**

## 7. Cost reality check vs. topicals

| Path | Cost | Session count | Realistic outcome |
|------|------|--------------|-------------------|
| Topical PDRN + barrier protocol | $26–$70 | Daily, 8+ weeks | Evenness, hydration, mild glow |
| Home "needling" kit | $12–$60 | Risky | Usually redness, sometimes damage |
| One professional treatment | $150–$500 | 1 | Modest, short-lived |
| Course of 3 professional sessions | $450–$1,500 | 3 | The real, visible change |

**The smart path for most people:** run the topical protocol for 8 weeks first. If the barrier is healthy and you still want more, *then* consider one professional treatment — and do it with a provider who talks about delivery, not magic.

---

## 8. Absolute contraindications (do not treat)

Do **not** get needling or laser if you:

- Are pregnant or breastfeeding (ask your provider first)
- Have active acne, eczema, psoriasis, or a rash in the area
- Have a keloid or hypertrophic-scar history
- Have taken isotretinoin (Accutane) in the last 6–12 months
- Have an active infection, cold sore, or open wound
- Have used acids/retinol within 5 days
- Are immunocompromised (consult your doctor)

**When in doubt, don't treat. Your barrier is the only thing you can't buy back.**
