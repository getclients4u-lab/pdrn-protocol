# The Product Tracker

### One new product at a time. Five-day gaps. Zero guessing.

---

## Why this worksheet exists

Almost every "my skin reacted" story has the same root cause: **three new products in two weeks**, then no idea which one broke the barrier.

This tracker enforces the only method that works:

> **One new product. Five-day gap. Log the result. Then — and only then — the next one.**

If you keep this tracker honestly for eight weeks, you will know more about your own skin than 99% of people who consume skincare content for years.

---

## How to use it

1. **Baseline week:** fill in the Baseline Snapshot below (before you change anything).
2. Every time you introduce something new — a PDRN, a moisturizer, a sunscreen — start a **Trial Block**.
3. Run it for **5 days** with nothing else new.
4. Score it on day 5. Keep it, pause it, or bin it.
5. Wait **at least 2 days** before the next trial.
6. Re-run the **Barrier Audit** every 30 days.

## The two-minute weekly review

Every Sunday, answer four questions:

1. Did I introduce **more than one** new product this week? (should be no)
2. Did anything sting, burn, or flush? (log it)
3. Am I seeing **change** or just hoping? (check photos, not memory)
4. Is my barrier score **8+** enough to keep my current stack? (if not, cut back)

---

## Progress photos — do it right

- **Same** time of day, **same** light, **same** distance
- No makeup, freshly cleansed, no product
- Weekly, not daily (daily photos show lighting, not skin)
- Store them in one album so you can scrub through

Memory is a liar. Photos are not.
