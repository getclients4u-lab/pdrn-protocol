### Printing optional. Following it, not optional.

---

## How to read this

- **Print this file** (skills: pandoc → PDF, see the pack README).
- One row per day. Check the box. Log the two scores.
- **Scores:** Tingle and Redness, 0–3 each (0 = none).
- **AM / PM columns** tell you what kind of night it is: `B` = barrier only, `P` = PDRN night, `R` = reset night.

**Tingle:** 0 none · 1 mild tingle · 2 noticeable · 3 burning (STOP)
**Redness:** 0 none · 1 slight · 2 visible · 3 inflamed (STOP)

## The Day-30 review

Answer these honestly:

1. **Barrier Audit score now:** ___/10 (vs. baseline ___)
2. **Tingle trend:** better / same / worse
3. **Redness trend:** better / same / worse
4. **Glow trend (compare photos, not memory):** better / same / worse
5. **Did I introduce more than one new product at a time?** yes / no
6. **Did I skip PDRN nights randomly?** yes / no

**What to do next:**

- Score **8–10** and glow **improved** → move to Phase 4 Maintain. Consider adding retinol on a separate night.
- Score **6–7** → hold the current stack another 30 days. Don't add anything.
- Score **≤ 5** → the barrier, not the PDRN, is the project. Full Reset. Re-read the Repair Playbook.

## The one-page habit

If you do nothing else with this pack, do this:

> **Every night: cleanse → (PDRN 2–3×/week) → moisturizer → sleep. Every morning: rinse → moisturizer → SPF.**

Consistency for 8 weeks beats a perfect routine for 5 days. The glow people chase on their feed is just *this*, repeated.
